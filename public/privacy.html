Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Nebula (“Nebula,” “we,” or “us”), the operator of this Service, handles information when you use this website and its school and personal workspace features.

Important about this prototype: accounts and most workspace information are stored in your browser, not in a Nebula account database. Community topics and replies are sent to and stored in the shared community database when that service is deployed and available. The current sign-in flow does not verify identities or school membership on the server, so forum names and organization scopes are not access controls. Do not use the Service for sensitive, confidential, or protected student information.

Information the Service uses

  • Account details: usernames, email addresses, passwords, organization details, profile settings, and active-session information you enter or create. These are stored in your browser’s local storage. In this prototype, passwords are not encrypted by Nebula before browser storage.
  • Workspace content: notes and documents, schedules and calendar events, profile choices, and virtual store or reward information are stored in local storage on your device. Community topics and replies you submit are stored in the shared community database. Personal accounts share a forum and organization forums are kept in separate data scopes, but the current API does not verify membership or restrict who can request a scope. Do not regard forum content as private.
  • Google sign-in details: if you choose Google sign-in, Google Identity Services may provide your name, email address, and a Google account identifier. The current client-side prototype uses those details to create or use a local account. Nebula does not operate a backend account database or send the Google sign-in credential to a Nebula server. Google processes information under its own policies.
  • Messages to us: if you email us, we receive the information and contact details in your message so we can respond.
  • Connection information: the web hosting provider may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser, and request time when your browser loads the site. The hosting provider’s own privacy terms apply to its processing.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, keep account and workspace state in your browser, share community topics and replies with the relevant forum, support Google sign-in when you choose it, respond to your messages, and maintain or protect the Service. The current Service does not use its own analytics or advertising system and does not sell personal information.

Local storage, access, and deletion

Local storage is saved on your device by your browser and can remain after you close the site. People or software with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored information. Do not use a shared or untrusted device for private information. Community topics and replies are stored separately in the shared database and are not removed when you clear browser storage or sign out.

You can remove locally stored Nebula information by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. This removes the locally stored account and workspace data for this browser and may not be recoverable. Signing out alone does not delete it. To request removal of a community post or reply, email us with enough detail to identify it. Nebula may not be able to retrieve or change account and workspace information that exists only in your browser.

Cookies and third-party services

Nebula’s current app features use browser local storage rather than Nebula-set tracking cookies. If you choose Google sign-in, Google Identity Services loads from Google and Google may use cookies or similar technologies under Google’s own privacy and cookie policies. The Service may link to third-party sites, including Google Classroom; those sites are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and international processing

We do not sell your personal information. Community topics and replies are shared with users of the relevant personal or organization forum. Information may also be handled by the site’s hosting and database provider, by Google when you use Google sign-in, when you direct us to share something, or when disclosure is required by law. Google and infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than Jamaica under their own terms. Nebula cannot access notes or account data that remain only in your browser.

Children and school use

Nebula is designed for school-related use, but the current prototype does not verify a user’s age, school authorization, or guardian consent. If you are a minor, use the Service only with the permission and supervision required by your parent or guardian and your school. Do not enter sensitive personal, health, financial, or confidential school information. Parents, guardians, and schools should assess whether this prototype is suitable before permitting a student to use it.

Your choices and applicable rights

You may choose not to use Google sign-in and may clear local storage as described above. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, including Jamaica’s Data Protection Act, you may have rights concerning personal information. Contact us to ask a question or make a request. Because accounts and most workspace information are stored locally, we may not have access to retrieve or change them for you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to use of the Service going forward, subject to applicable law.
